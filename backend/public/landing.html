Fairness Rotation Engine

Churches often accidentally schedule the same 3 musicians every week while others get left out. ChurchFlow tracks serving history and picks qualified volunteers who haven't served recently—with zero double-booking.

2. No WhatsApp Chasing

1-Tap Attendance Link

Instead of texting 10 people individually, share one service link in your worship WhatsApp group. Volunteers open the link on their phone and tap "I'm Playing" in 2 seconds—no app install or password needed.

3. No Friday Night Panic

Self-Healing Auto-Roster

When a drummer or guitarist taps "Can't Make It", ChurchFlow doesn't leave an empty hole on stage. It immediately promotes the next most fair, available volunteer for that position automatically.
